Find

Roles that fit, scored and sorted.

  • Hourly discovery from company job boards on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby.
  • A fit score for every posting against your resume. Top matches get a text.
  • A board by stage, from queued to offer, with search and filters.

Tailor

One resume per posting. Nothing made up.

  • Built only from your own lines. It selects, reorders and rephrases real experience.
  • Keyword coverage shows which of the posting’s asks each version answers.
  • A matching cover letter and a one-page PDF, checked before it goes out.

Apply

It fills the form. You press Submit.

  • Assisted apply on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby forms, watched live on screen.
  • Never guesses on work authorization, pay or demographic questions. Those come only from your saved answers.
  • Auto-submit is off by default. You solve any CAPTCHA and make the final click.

Track

Every reply caught. Every follow-up drafted.

  • Watches your inbox for replies, interview invites and bounced emails.
  • Drafts responses by your rules: follow-ups after a quiet week, thank-yous after interviews.
  • Nothing sends on its own. You read each draft and press Send.

Prepare

Walk into every interview ready.

  • A game plan per role: likely interview steps, key dates and a plan of attack.
  • A skill builder for each skill the posting asks for that your resume does not show yet.
  • Practice and a coach, plus a printable prep pack. Every estimate shows its source.

Network

See who can open a door.

  • Your own LinkedIn export shows who you know at the companies you track.
  • A referral ask drafted for each contact, ready to copy.
  • No scraping and no bots. The file stays on your computer.

Your agent

A copilot that knows the whole pipeline.

  • Ask anything: what to focus on this week, which skill opens the most postings.
  • Suggestions arrive as cards. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.
  • Bring your own model and run a built-in check before relying on it.
